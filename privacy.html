Privacy Policy for PrivCite

Effective Date: May 28, 2026
Last Updated: September 9, 2026

PrivCite is a local-first macOS application designed to help law students, law clerks, summer associates, and attorneys review legal documents for Bluebook citation compliance and writing style issues.

Data Collection and Use

PrivCite itself collects no personal data or document content.

  • All document analysis, issue detection, redlining, and export operations occur on your device using Apple frameworks and Swift code.
  • Your documents, memos, briefs, and any generated review or fixed copies never leave your Mac and are never sent to Andrew Wesley Dodson or any PrivCite server. PrivCite has no backend.
  • There are no PrivCite accounts, no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising.
  • The app does not require a network connection to analyze documents or export Word files.

Optional CourtListener Lookup

By default, PrivCite makes no outbound network connections.

If you choose to paste a CourtListener API token in Preferences, PrivCite may send only standalone public citation coordinates (for example, 372 U.S. 335) to CourtListener so it can verify the case name. It does not send your document, party names from the brief, client information, or any other work-product text.

This lookup is optional, off by default, and can be left blank. CourtListener’s own privacy policy applies to data you send to that service.

Your Rights

Because PrivCite never receives or stores your documents, there is nothing for us to access, delete, or correct on our end. You retain complete control over your work at all times.

Third-Party Services

PrivCite contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing SDKs. The only optional third-party contact is CourtListener, and only if you supply your own API token.

Changes to This Policy

We will update this policy if the app’s behavior changes. The last-updated date will be revised accordingly. Past analyses performed on your machine are unaffected.
