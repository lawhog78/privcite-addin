PrivCite

A local-first Bluebook citation and legal writing checker for macOS.

PrivCite analyzes memos and briefs on your Mac for citation, spacing, grammar/legal-style, and passive-voice issues. It can export a redlined Word review copy with real comments, or a cleaned-up fixed copy. Your documents never leave the device.

Support

  • Bug reports and feature requests: GitHub Issues
  • Privacy policy: privacy.html

What it does

  • Bluebook-aware citation checks (cases, statutes, Id./short forms, signals, explanatory phrases)
  • Spacing, grammar & legal style, and passive-voice flags
  • Export a Review copy with Microsoft Word comments, or a Fixed copy with changes applied
  • Optional CourtListener lookup (off by default) that sends only public citation coordinates, never document text

PrivCite is an assistant, not a substitute for The Bluebook or careful human review.
